# Special Abilities

> Categorization of non-spell abilities that creatures, classes, and items can use: Spell-like, Supernatural, Extraordinary, Natural.

## Purpose of categorization

Spells follow well-defined rules (components, casting time, AoO, SR, etc.). Many creature and class powers are **not** spells but produce magical or extraordinary effects. The four categories tell the GM and players how each ability interacts with magic-related rules: counterspells, antimagic, AoOs, SR.

## Spell-like abilities (Sp)

- Behave like spells, but with key differences:
  - **No verbal, somatic, or material components** — never. Caster activates them mentally.
  - **No XP cost** ever, even if the equivalent spell has one.
  - **No focus or divine focus** required.
  - The user's armor doesn't impose Arcane Spell Failure (no S component).
- **Casting time**: 1 standard action, unless the description states otherwise.
- **Provoke AoO** when used (just like casting a spell in melee).
- **Subject to spell resistance** (the equivalent spell's SR rules apply).
- Can be **counterspelled** (the equivalent spell counters; *dispel magic* works as counter).
- Can be **dispelled** by *dispel magic*.
- Function in an **antimagic field** like spells: **suppressed/blocked**.
- Save DCs based on the creature's relevant ability score (typically Cha for monsters): `10 + spell level + Cha mod`.
- Caster level for Sp abilities is the creature's HD unless specified otherwise.
- Examples: dragon's *suggestion*; devil's *teleport*; succubus's *charm monster*; many monster spell-likes.

## Supernatural abilities (Su)

- Magical in nature but **not** spells.
- **No components** of any kind; activated mentally.
- Usually **standard action** unless described otherwise; may be free or move.
- **Do NOT provoke AoO** (typical) — read the ability for exceptions.
- **Not subject to spell resistance** — SR has no effect.
- **Cannot be counterspelled** (no spell to counter).
- **Cannot be dispelled** by *dispel magic*.
- **Suppressed in an antimagic field** (do not function inside one).
- Examples: dragon's breath weapon; medusa's petrifying gaze; cleric's turn/rebuke undead; spectre's energy drain; troll's regeneration.

## Extraordinary abilities (Ex)

- **Not magical**. Reflect natural traits, training, or biology that defies normal logic but isn't supernatural.
- **No components**.
- Action type per description (often free or part of normal actions).
- **Do NOT provoke AoO**.
- **Not subject to SR**.
- **Cannot be counterspelled** or dispelled.
- **Function normally in an antimagic field** — they're not magic.
- Examples: rogue's Evasion; barbarian's Rage; monk's Flurry of Blows; uncanny dodge; most fighter combat feats; ranger's combat style. (Note: troll regeneration is **Su**, not Ex.)

## Natural abilities

- A creature's **innate physical traits** that aren't otherwise categorized as Ex/Su/Sp.
- Bird's flight, fish's swim, etc.
- Function in any environment (including antimagic fields) that physically permits.
- Function under any conditions that physically permit (e.g. darkness, water, fall).

## Comparison table

|  | Spell-like (Sp) | Supernatural (Su) | Extraordinary (Ex) | Natural |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Components | None (mental) | None | None | n/a |
| Provokes AoO when used | **Yes** (like a spell) | No (typically) | No | No |
| Subject to SR | **Yes** | No | No | No |
| Can be counterspelled | **Yes** | No | No | No |
| Suppressed by antimagic | **Yes** | **Yes** | No | No |
| Dispellable by *dispel magic* | **Yes** | No | No | No |
| Save DC formula | `10 + spell-level + Cha (or relevant)` | per ability | per ability or n/a | n/a |

## Use considerations

- A creature that has both Sp and Su abilities navigates antimagic carefully: Su shuts off too, but it doesn't lose AC or natural attacks (Ex/Natural).
- A monk's wholeness of body / abundant step / empty body progression spans Su; their basic combat abilities (Flurry, AC bonus, Slow Fall) are Ex.
- A paladin's lay-on-hands is Su (no components); turn undead is Su; smite evil is Su (functions in antimagic? **No** — Su shut off in antimagic).
- A cleric attempting to turn undead in antimagic: **fails** (Su).
- A barbarian raging in antimagic: works (Ex).
- A dragon's breath in antimagic: **fails** (Su).

## Concentration and special abilities

- Sp abilities: like casting a spell; Concentration checks apply if interrupted (DC same as a spell of the equivalent level).
- Su / Ex / Natural: not interruptible the same way; resolve regardless.
