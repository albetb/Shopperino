# Combat Maneuvers

> Special melee actions: charge, two-weapon fighting, mounted combat, disarm, feint, splash weapons, overrun, trip, bull rush, sunder. Grapple is intentionally omitted (rarely used in this campaign).

## Charge

- **Full-round action**. Move up to 2× speed in a straight line and make one melee attack at the end.
- **Movement requirement**: minimum **3 m (2 sq)**, maximum 2× speed; clear, unobstructed path; level terrain (no difficult terrain).
- The first square from which you can attack the target must be clear; obstructions or allies in the line break the charge.
- **Bonus**: +2 to the charge attack roll, **−2 AC** until your next turn.
- Cannot make a 5-ft step in the same round as a charge.
- If restricted to 1 standard or 1 move that round (slowed, surprise), you can still **partial charge**: standard action; up to **base speed** of movement, single attack, +2/−2 as normal.
- A **lance** wielded from a charging mount deals **double damage**.
- **Spears**, **ranseurs**, and similar braced piercing weapons set against a charger deal double damage to the charger.
- Charge ends adjacent to the target. Can't charge a target you can't see (no line of sight).
- Can be used to initiate **Bull Rush** (in place of attack) or **Overrun** (during the move).

## Two-weapon fighting

Wielding a weapon in each hand grants one extra attack with the off-hand each round. Penalties:

| Circumstance | Main hand | Off hand |
|---|---|---|
| Default | −6 | −10 |
| Off-hand is light | −4 | −8 |
| Two-Weapon Fighting feat | −4 | −4 |
| Off-hand is light + Two-Weapon Fighting feat | −2 | −2 |

- An **unarmed strike** counts as a light weapon for these penalties.
- **Double weapons** (two-bladed sword, quarterstaff): treat the second end as a light weapon in the off-hand; same penalty table.
- **Throwing weapons** in each hand: same rules. Shuriken count as ammo (not thrown weapons) but use these rules. Bolas/javelin/net + rope = one-handed.
- The extra attack from two-weapon fighting is **only** during a full-attack action.
- Improved/Greater Two-Weapon Fighting feats grant additional off-hand attacks at progressively lower bonuses (off-hand iteratives).

## Mounted combat

- **Mounting / dismounting** = move action; **Ride DC 20** for free action mount/dismount; failure = move action wasted.
- The mount uses the **rider's initiative**; the rider acts on their turn, mount moves at the rider's discretion.
- **Combatants**: warhorses, war ponies = combat-trained; light/draft horses are not. Untrained mount in combat → Ride DC 20 (move action) per round to control, else can't perform other actions.
- **Mounted on a Large+ mount**: rider gains **+1 to melee attack** from the height advantage.
- **Mount's movement vs rider's actions**:
  - Mount moves up to 1.5 m (5 ft) → rider may make full-attack.
  - Mount moves more than 1.5 m → rider gets a single attack (standard).
  - Mount runs (×4) → rider takes **−4** to attacks (single attack only; ranged attacks can be made).
  - Mount charges → rider gets the +2/−2 charge bonus and the lance double-damage rule.
- **Ranged weapons while mounted**: time the shot to coincide with the moment the mount has completed half its move; even then, mounted ranged at run = −4 (already accounted for).
- **Spellcasting on a moving mount**: Concentration check DC `10 + spell level` (mount moves up to 2× speed) or DC `15 + spell level` (mount runs).
- **Mount falls in combat**: rider Ride DC 15 to land standing (else takes 1d6, prone).
- **Rider falls/drops**: 50% chance to stay in saddle (75% with a war saddle); else falls, prone, 1d6.
- **Fight with knees** (Ride DC 5): both hands free for one round (free action). Failure = use both hands on reins this round.

## Disarm

- Melee attack action that attempts to knock a weapon from a foe's hand. **Provokes an AoO** (unless Improved Disarm feat).
- **If unarmed disarming**: also takes the AoO above; if disarm fails, action ends (no normal attack).
- **Step 1**: Suffer the AoO (Improved Disarm avoids).
- **Step 2**: **Opposed attack rolls** (each makes an attack roll with their weapon).
  - Two-handed wielder: **+4** to the roll.
  - Light weapon: **−4**.
  - Larger weapon (one size up): **+4**.
  - The disarming creature gains/loses by size: **+4 per size category larger**, **−4 per size smaller**.
- **Step 3**: Win → defender's weapon falls in their square. Lose → if you were armed and the defender chooses, you may be **counter-disarmed** by them as a free action (no AoO this time).
- A disarmed creature on its turn may pick the weapon up (move action provoking AoO).
- **Holding the weapon with sure-grip** (e.g. locked gauntlet, weapon-cord): defender +10 to opposed roll to keep it.
- **Grabbing an item** (worn but not wielded, e.g. amulet): like a disarm but use a touch attack instead of opposed weapon attack. Targets get +4 to oppose if the item is firmly held.

## Feint

- **Standard action**. Bluff check vs target's Sense Motive (or `10 + BAB + Wis mod`, whichever higher).
- Success → on your next attack against that target before your next turn, the target loses Dex to AC.
- **Modifiers**:
  - Non-humanoid: **−4** to Bluff.
  - Animal-Int (1–2): **−8**.
  - Mindless / non-animal Int (0): cannot feint.
- Feinting does **not** provoke.
- **Improved Feint** feat: feint as a move action.

## Splash weapons (alchemist's fire, holy water, acid)

- Ranged attack against the **target square's grid intersection** at **AC 5** (no Str/Dex/proficiency penalty applies).
- **Direct hit on a creature**: ranged touch attack vs that creature; on hit, full damage; adjacent squares take splash damage.
- **Targeting an intersection**: hit on AC 5 → no creature takes direct damage but all creatures in adjacent squares take splash damage.
- **Miss the target square**: roll **1d8** to determine direction (clock face), then **1 + (1 per range increment thrown)** to determine how many squares off-line. The flask lands at that point and splashes that intersection.
- Splash damage typically = **1** (for fire/acid/holy water) or specific to item.
- No proficiency for splash weapons; no Str bonus to damage.

## Overrun

- Done as a **standard action** during a move (or as part of a charge move). Attempt to move through a foe's square.
- **Step 1**: Provokes an AoO from the defender (Improved Overrun feat avoids).
- **Step 2**: Defender chooses to **avoid** (no effect — you continue) or to **block**.
  - Avoiding ends the attempt without contest.
  - Blocking → opposed Str / Dex check (defender's choice).
  - Modifiers: **+4 per size category** the larger creature exceeds the smaller; defender +4 if it has more than two legs or is otherwise extremely stable.
  - Charging → +2 to your roll.
  - Mounted → use mount's Str.
- **Step 3**:
  - Win → foe is knocked **prone**, you continue your move.
  - Lose & fall → you are knocked prone in your previous square (movement ends).
  - Lose without falling → you stop 1.5 m (1 sq) before the defender (movement ends).
- **Improved Overrun**: defender cannot choose to avoid.
- **Trampling** (a separate special attack): some large creatures may overrun multiple foes per round and deal damage automatically as part of their movement (creature-specific).

## Trip

- **Touch attack** in melee (does not deal damage). **Provokes** unless Improved Trip.
- **Step 1**: Suffer AoO (Improved Trip avoids).
- **Step 2**: Make a melee touch attack vs the target. On hit:
- **Step 3**: Opposed **Str** check (you) vs **Str** or **Dex** (defender's choice).
  - **+4 per size category** difference (larger gets +4).
  - Quadrupeds / extremely stable creatures: **+4** on the check (cumulatively for many legs / lower center).
- Win → defender is **prone**.
- **Counter-trip**: if you fail the Str check, defender may immediately attempt a free counter-trip against you (no AoO, no touch attack — just opposed Str check; if they win, you fall prone).
- **Improved Trip**: no AoO, +4 to opposed check, free attack at full BAB against tripped foe.
- Some weapons (whip, flail, guisarme, spiked chain) allow a trip attempt and let you drop the weapon to avoid being counter-tripped if you fail.

## Bull rush (Spingere)

- **Standard action** (or substituted for the attack at the end of a charge).
- **Step 1**: Move into defender's square. **Provokes AoO** from defender and from any other foe whose threatened squares you cross (Improved Bull Rush avoids the defender's AoO).
- **Step 2**: Opposed **Str** checks.
  - **+4 per size category** larger; **−4 per smaller**.
  - Charging: **+2**.
  - Defender +4 if more than two legs or otherwise stable.
- **Step 3**: Win → defender pushed back **1.5 m (1 sq)**, plus **+1.5 m per 5** points by which you exceed the contested check. You move with the defender to keep them adjacent (no extra movement allowed beyond your normal limit).
- Defender's pushed movement provokes AoOs from anyone other than you.
- Lose → you are pushed back 1.5 m (1 sq) into your previous square (or fall prone if blocked).
- Bull rush can only target a creature **one size smaller, equal, or one size larger**.

## Sunder (Spezzare)

- Melee attack against a wielded weapon or shield. **Provokes AoO** unless Improved Sunder.
- **Step 1**: AoO from defender (avoid with Improved Sunder).
- **Step 2**: **Opposed attack rolls**. Two-handed wielder gets **+4**; light weapon **−4**; **+4 per size larger**.
- **Step 3**: Win → roll damage. Compare against the item's hardness and HP (Table 8-10 — see source for typical values; e.g. Medium longsword = hardness 10, 5 HP; heavy steel shield = hardness 10, 20 HP). Reduce HP by `damage − hardness` (min 0). Item destroyed at 0 HP.
- **Sundering an attended object** (worn, not wielded, like cloak or glasses): standard attack vs `AC 10 + size mod + Dex mod` of bearer; provokes AoO; bearer's worn armor doesn't help.
- Cannot sunder armor someone is wearing this way.

## Aid another (combat use)

- Standard action. Attack vs **AC 10**; on success ally gets **+2 attack** OR **+2 AC** vs that opponent on their next attack/the rest of their next turn.
- Multiple aiders' bonuses stack.
- Also has skill-check and saving-throw applications (see [skills.md](skills.md)).
